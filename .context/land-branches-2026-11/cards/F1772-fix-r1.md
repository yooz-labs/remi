3. Contract Minor and Maintainability Minor: `entries` is `Array(self)` with no reserved capacity (repeated doubling, a `Node` copy per element); the doc comment's wording ("O(1) UUID lookup" for an internal dictionary, "compacted occasionally" without the rule, "the order MachineStore has historically exposed" as history, `entries` allocating an O(n) copy per call).
