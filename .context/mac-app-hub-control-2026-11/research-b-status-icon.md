## 1. What exists today (verified)

- A menu bar extra already ships: `MenuBarExtra` with `.menuBarExtraStyle(.window)` (`Mac/RemiMacApp.swift:33-43`), whose label is `MacMenuBarLabel` (`Mac/MacNotificationCoordinator.swift:246`) and whose content is the needs-you card list (`Mac/MacLiveNeedsYouMenu.swift`).
- The icon is `RemiMenuBarIcon` (`RemiKit/Sources/RemiUI/MenuBarIcon.swift:18-43`): an 18 by 18 pt template image, a 16 pt rounded square, the brand "r" (`r-mask.png`, 9 pt tall) inside; thin outline while nothing waits, filled square with the "r" knocked out while something does, the count beside it (`MacNotificationCoordinator.swift:279-320`).
- `RemiMenuBarStatus` (`RemiUI/MenuBarStatus.swift`) holds only `pendingCount` and `identityAvailable`, and the VoiceOver label ("Remi, 2 pending requests"). Without an identity the icon is the SF Symbol `exclamationmark.triangle`.
- Design rules: DESIGN.md:30 (the icon "stays", one color, outline versus fill), DESIGN.md:127-128 (lime is the attention accent only; "every other status uses neutral tones"), DESIGN.md:58 and VISUAL-ACCEPTANCE.md:35 (never color alone), BRAND-ASSETS.md:44 (the letter is never redrawn; regenerate the mask from the canonical asset).
- The app is sandboxed with `network.client` only (`Mac/Remi.entitlements:5-8`) and is a client of hubs; it cannot read `pmset`. Keep-awake state can reach it only over the connection.
- Keep-awake is not on the wire: no `awake` string exists in `packages/shared/src` (grep, empty). ADR 0050 slice 4 and plan card L2-APP (outline, branch `docs/issue-1773-awake-level2-plan`) name a future `keep_awake_status` message and `keep_awake` capability, and a phone start needs `allow_phone = true` (ADR 0050 table row, line 185). Level 1 is per session daemon and the hub holds nothing (`docs/agent-reference/keep-awake.md:12`).
- Available today to the app: per-machine connection status (`MachineConnectionStatus`: disconnected, connecting, connected, waitingForApproval, unavailable; `RemiKit/Store/MachineStore.swift:133`), session status (`RemiSessionStatus.working` and others, `RemiUI/PresentationModels.swift:171`), `RemiNeedsYouAvailability` (unconfigured, offline, partially connected, connected; `RemiUI/NeedsYouAvailability.swift`) and a `hubSupport` mirror (`RemiKit/Protocol/ProtocolSupport.swift`).

## 2. The states, and what the plan changes

The level 2 plan (L2-POL, plan.md section 2, commit `c7ab6f51`) has the policy states `disabled`, `armed`, `disarmed`, `starting`, `on`, `reverting`, plus a `lastRefusal` on an armed hub that an automatic start was refused.
There is no "paused, waiting to resume": any revert (battery, thermal, foreign holder, expiry) ends in `disarmed`, "never back to `armed`" (L2-POL item 9).
The nearest real state is **armed with a refusal** (battery low, thermal, another app holding the Mac awake): the hub waits and tries again at the next work episode.
The icon should show that, and must not promise a resume that the plan does not do.

Proposed final set (12; the owner's 11 with two merges and one addition):

| # | State | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hub not running, unreachable or version mismatch | no machine connected | one glyph; a sandboxed client cannot tell "not running" from "unreachable" (both a failed connect); the menu says which |
| 2 | Error | identity unavailable (exists today) | triangle |
| 3 | Hub up, nothing happening | connected, no work | exists today |
| 4 | Sessions working, level 1 holding | session `working`, level 1 hold | see below |
| 5 | A session needs you | pending count above 0 | exists today, with count |
| 6 | Needs you and level 1 holding | 4 and 5 together | the two dimensions are independent |
| 7 | Level 2 armed, waiting for work | `armed` | |
| 8 | Level 2 armed, start blocked by a condition | `armed` with `lastRefusal` | replaces "paused, waiting to resume" |
| 9 | Level 2 active, lid closed possible | `starting`, `on`, `reverting` | the stakes are highest here: a laptop awake in a bag |
| 10 | Level 2 active and needs you | 5 and 9 | |
| 11 | Level 2 ended by a failure | revert reason a failure (`switch-failed`, `heartbeat-failed`, `lease-write-failed`) | shown until the person has seen it; a stop or an expiry is not a failure |
| 12 | Level 2 disarmed after your stop | `disarmed` | looks like state 3; the menu says "disarmed" |

Decisions inside the set:
- **Working is not a separate mark.** Level 1 holds while a session has real work, plus a 120 s grace (`keep-awake.md:15-23`), so with level 1 on (the default) "level 1 holding" already says "something is working". With `level1 = false`, or off macOS, working shows only in the menu and tooltip. DESIGN.md principle 1 ("calm by default", color and motion only for the moment an agent needs them) points the same way. An optional extra mark for working is a cost without a decision behind it.
- **Keep-awake describes the local Mac only.** The Mac app lists many machines, but a status icon in this Mac's bar speaks for this Mac; the hub on loopback is the one it reflects. Other machines' wake state belongs in the menu.
- **Precedence of the base glyph:** error, then no machine connected (use `RemiNeedsYouAvailability.offline`, not "any machine offline"), then needs you, then idle. A needs-you card stays the loudest state, as DESIGN.md:30 says.
- **Level 1 and level 2 together:** show level 2 (while level 2 is on, the level 1 hold is moot).
- The icon states of 4 and 7 to 12 are blocked on the wire (L2-APP). States 1 to 3, 5 and 12 are buildable now.

## 3. What Apple says (questions 1 and 2)

HIG, "The menu bar", Menu bar extras (read 2026-11-17, `.../design/human-interface-guidelines/the-menu-bar.json`):
- "Both interface icons and symbols use black and clear colors to define their shapes; the system can apply other colors to the black areas in each image so it looks good on both dark and light menu bars, and when your menu bar extra is selected." This is the template contract.
- "The menu bar's height is 24 pt." (Measured here: `NSStatusBar.system.thickness` printed `22.0` in a command-line process on macOS 27.2; whether the value differs with a notch or an attached display is UNVERIFIED. The 18 pt canvas fits either.)
- "Display a menu, not a popover, when people click your menu bar extra." "Unless the app functionality you want to expose is too complex for a menu, avoid presenting it in a popover." Remi's `.window` style is a popover; the question cards (buttons, free text, structured answers) are the "too complex" case, so it stays. New rows for hub and keep-awake belong in that same window.
- "Let people, not your app, decide whether to put your menu bar extra in the menu bar" and "Avoid relying on the presence of menu bar extras", "the system hides and shows menu bar extras regularly", "if there are too many menu bar extras, the system may hide some". The same page suggests a Dock menu as the always-available path. Consequences: a wide sign can be the first thing hidden; the level 2 safety state must also be reachable elsewhere (a Dock menu, a notification when a period starts and ends, which plan Q11 already wants); and the app has no show/hide setting for its extra today (`MenuBarExtra` without `isInserted`; the SwiftUI page says `isInserted` is set to false when the user removes it).
- The HIG page gives no guidance on text in a status item, on badges, or on accessibility.

`NSImage.isTemplate` (`.../appkit/nsimage/istemplate.json`): "Images you mark as template images should consist of only black and clear colors. You can use the alpha channel in the image to adjust the opacity of black content." A template is one color; a colored badge cannot live inside one.

`NSStatusItem` (`.../appkit/nsstatusitem.json`): `length` is the space allocated; `variableLength` "dynamically adjusts to the width of its contents"; `squareLength` equals the bar thickness; the `button` customizes appearance. `NSStatusBarButton.appearsDisabled`: "a disabled/off appearance while still being functional". The page documents no maximum width.

`MenuBarExtra` (`.../swiftui/menubarextra.json`): macOS 13+, a label closure takes any `View`; the string and symbol initializers use the title "for the accessibility system". `.window` "renders its contents in a popover-like window".

SF Symbols (HIG, `.../sf-symbols.json`): four rendering modes (monochrome, hierarchical, palette, multicolor); "using system-provided colors ensures that symbols automatically adapt to accessibility accommodations and appearance modes"; variable color is for "a characteristic that can change over time, like capacity or strength", not for depth; animations "judiciously" (pulse for ongoing activity, variable color for "playback, connecting, or broadcasting"). A palette or multicolor symbol is not a template, so the bar does not tint it.

Accessibility (HIG, `.../accessibility.json` and `.../color.json`):
- "Offer visual indicators, like distinct shapes or icons, in addition to color to help people perceive differences in function and changes in state"; "Avoid relying solely on color to differentiate between objects ... you can use text labels or glyph shapes to identify objects or states."
- Reduce Motion: "reducing automatic and repetitive animations, including zooming, scaling, and peripheral motion"; `NSWorkspace.accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion` is the AppKit flag (macOS 10.12+).
- Differentiate Without Color: `accessibilityDifferentiateWithoutColor` (SwiftUI environment, macOS 10.15+): "UI should not convey information using color alone and instead should use shapes or glyphs".
- Increase Contrast: "ensure it at least provides a higher contrast color scheme when the system setting Increase Contrast is turned on"; AppKit flag `accessibilityDisplayShouldIncreaseContrast` (macOS 10.10+).
- VoiceOver: label interface elements; the HIG page has nothing specific to a status item.

Not found in Apple's pages, so UNVERIFIED: a maximum status item width; two-line text support. Forum reports (read as search summaries only, not the threads): a two-line `attributedTitle` was clipped from macOS 11 on and needed a paragraph-style line-height cap; the `MenuBarExtra` label "works poorly for anything other than SF Symbols or text" and may not re-render on `@State` changes (2023); animation in a status item is choppy when it hosts SwiftUI scroll content; a thread titled "Various menu bar NSStatusItem issues with macOS 27" exists (`origin-devforums.apple.com/forums/thread/836113`, title only, unread). The shipping label already uses an `NSImage` plus a `Text` (`MacNotificationCoordinator.swift:293-317`), which is the shape that works; a stacked sign drawn into the template image avoids text layout entirely.

How many states are legible at 18 pt (from the renderings, 36 px, both bars, `icon-concepts/sheet.svg`):
- Four base glyphs are clearly different by shape: outline, filled with knock-out, dashed, triangle.
- A 4.5 pt corner badge (9 px at 2x) shows five shapes: dot, ring, half-filled ring, flat pill, cross. Dot, pill and cross read at once; ring versus half-ring is marginal at 1x, so badges alone should carry at most three or four meanings.
- A sign of two 7.4 pt bold caps lines (about 5.2 pt cap height if SF Pro's cap-height ratio is 0.705, recalled and UNVERIFIED) is crisp at 2x and carries any number of states, at the price of about 20 to 28 pt of width.
- Lime contrast (my calculation from the `RemiTheme.Color.attention` value, `Theme.swift:10-14`): 1.15 to 1.36 to 1 on a light bar, 10.9 to 1 on a dark bar. As a mark on a light bar it fails the 3 to 1 non-text rule that WCAG 2.2 (recalled, UNVERIFIED) sets; the shipped icon avoids this by using fill, not lime. Keep it that way.

## 4. Directions

Geometry shared by all: 18 pt canvas, the existing 16 pt square and 9 pt "r"; a keep-awake mark sits in the lower-right corner in a 4.5 pt cell with a 1 pt clear halo cut out of the glyph (the same `destinationOut` technique the filled state already uses, `MenuBarIcon.swift:82`), where the "r" has no ink; the count and any sign sit to the right, in that order.
The hub-down glyph is the outline dashed with the "r" at 55 percent alpha (alpha is allowed in a template).

Master state table (cells are what is drawn; "none" means the same as state 3):

| State | A badge, template | B colored light | C two-line sign | D SF Symbols | E badge L1, sign L2 (recommended) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 hub down | dashed square, dim "r" | same | same | `wifi.slash` | same as A |
| 2 error | triangle | same | same | `exclamationmark.triangle` | same as A |
| 3 idle | outline | outline | outline | `r.square` | outline |
| 4 working, L1 | dot | green dot | WAKE / ON | `cup.and.saucer.fill` | dot |
| 5 needs you | fill + "2" | fill + "2" | fill + "2" | `r.square.fill` + "2" | fill + "2" |
| 6 needs + L1 | fill, dot, "2" | fill, green dot, "2" | fill, "2", WAKE / ON | `r.square.fill`, "2", cup | fill, dot, "2" |
| 7 L2 armed | ring | blue ring | WAKE / ARM | `laptopcomputer` | WAKE / ARM |
| 8 L2 armed, blocked | half ring | yellow half ring | WAKE / WAIT | `pause.circle` | WAKE / WAIT |
| 9 L2 active | flat pill (a closed lid) | orange pill | WAKE / LID | `laptopcomputer.slash` | WAKE / LID |
| 10 L2 active + needs | fill, pill, "2" | fill, orange pill, "2" | fill, "2", WAKE / LID | `r.square.fill`, "2", `laptopcomputer.slash` | fill, "2", WAKE / LID |
| 11 L2 failed | cross | red cross | WAKE / FAIL | `xmark.circle` | WAKE / FAIL |

| Animation | none | none | none | optional pulse | none |
| Width (pt) | 18 to 29 | 18 to 29 | 18 to about 55 | 18 to about 47 | 18 to about 55 |

Text in the C and E signs ("WAKE", "ARM", "WAIT", "LID", "FAIL") is a first proposal; wording and localization are open (section 7).
The D symbol names were checked to exist on macOS 27.2 (`NSImage(systemSymbolName:)`), not for meaning; `r.square` is a capital "R" in the system font, not the brand letter, and `laptopcomputer.slash` reads as "no laptop".

### A. Monochrome template glyph with a state badge
- Pros: one template image, so macOS tints it for light and dark bars and the pressed state (HIG); same family as today's icon; no color, so it obeys DESIGN.md:128 and is color-blind safe by construction; smallest width; smallest change (one new parameter on `RemiMenuBarIcon.image`).
- Cons: ring versus half ring is marginal at 1x; the badge breaks the square's corner; five badge shapes to learn; level 2 (the dangerous state) is as small as level 1.
- Legibility: dot, pill and cross good in light and dark; ring and half ring marginal.
- Effort: low. `MenuBarExtra` label as now.

### B. Colored light
- Pros: the "light" the owner floated, and the quickest to read at a glance for sighted people.
- Cons: needs a non-template image, so the glyph must be drawn in the bar's own foreground color, and the bar's appearance can follow the wallpaper, not the system appearance (UNVERIFIED, so a wrong guess draws a black "r" on a dark bar); a colored mark is exempt from the highlighted-state tinting (the HIG says the system recolors the black areas; a colored area is not black); yellow on a light bar and green on a light bar have low contrast (the same arithmetic as lime); it spends color that DESIGN.md:128 reserves, and weakens the one-accent rule; color-blind safety rests on the same shapes as A, so color adds nothing a person who cannot see it can use; users with a themed or tinted bar may dislike colored status items (UNVERIFIED).
- Effort: medium to high (appearance handling, two palettes, Increase Contrast and Differentiate Without Color branches, plus A's shapes anyway).
- Not recommended.

### C. Two-line sign for every keep-awake state
- Pros: words carry the state, so it is unambiguous and works for color-blind users; closest to the owner's "WAKE ON".
- Cons: wide (about 55 pt with a count) in the most common keep-awake state, level 1, which holds whenever any agent works, so it would sit in the bar all day and break "calm by default"; text in an image does not localize; 7.4 pt text is small; the item is the first the system hides when the bar is crowded (HIG, "too many").
- Effort: medium (drawing text into the template image, or a two-line button title, with the Big Sur clipping reports UNVERIFIED).

### D. SF Symbol based
- Pros: the lowest cost (`MenuBarExtra("Remi", systemImage:)`), system rendering modes, symbol effects (pulse for "armed", variable color for time left) and system accessibility behavior for free.
- Cons: the brand "r" is gone in the keep-awake states, so the item stops looking like Remi exactly when the person needs to trust it; symbol meanings are weak (`laptopcomputer.slash`, a cup for "awake"); each state is a different glyph, so a two-dimension state (needs you and level 2) needs two symbols side by side; the HIG says animation is for ongoing activity and Reduce Motion must be honored.
- Effort: lowest.

### E. Badge for level 1, sign only for level 2 (recommended)
- Pros: the quiet state (level 1, the common one) costs a 4.5 pt dot, and only the rare, risky state (level 2: the Mac stays awake with the lid closed, on battery) pays for width and words, which is where a mistake is expensive; every state is shape or text, never color; one template image, so tinting and the highlighted state work; ring and half ring, the marginal shapes, are replaced by words; the needs-you contract (fill and count) is untouched; the sign needs no text layout if drawn into the image.
- Cons: two systems (badge and sign) to maintain; the sign is wide (about 55 pt with a count) and can be hidden when the bar is crowded; the sign text does not localize (use words that are short in the app's languages, or letters, section 7); level 2 states stay behind the wire work (L2-APP).
- Legibility: sign crisp at 2x on both bars; dot good.
- Effort: low to medium. Extend `RemiMenuBarStatus` to hold `hub`, `awake` and `sign`, extend `RemiMenuBarIcon.image` to take them, add alpha-bitmap tests in the style of `MenuBarIconTests`, and the previews gallery (`MenuBarIcon.swift:116-154`).

## 5. Recommendation

E, built in two steps.
Step 1, no wire change: the base glyphs (hub-down dashed, error triangle, idle outline, needs-you fill and count) and the new VoiceOver strings.
Step 2, with L2-APP: the level 1 dot and the level 2 sign, driven by `keep_awake_status`.
Keep lime out of the bar; color stays a possible later accent only after a hands-on look at a real light bar.

Why not "colored": the owner asked for something good for color-blind users, and the guidance (HIG) is that color is a redundant channel; shapes and words are enough, and a monochrome template is the only form the system tints for light, dark and pressed states.
The owner's "WAKE ON" idea is kept, for level 2 only, where it pays.

Whether the sign should say WAKE or something shorter is the owner's call (section 7, question 1).

## 6. Accessibility rules for the implementation

- Never color alone: every state differs by shape or words (A, C, E satisfy this by construction). If B is ever added, branch on `accessibilityDifferentiateWithoutColor` and on Increase Contrast.
- VoiceOver: the label is "Remi"; the value is one sentence per state, so a person hears state, not a symbol. Proposed values:
  1: "Remi, no hub reachable"; 2: "Remi unavailable" (today's string); 3: "Remi, nothing needs you"; 4: "Remi, sessions working, keeping this Mac awake"; 5: "Remi, 2 requests need you"; 7: "Remi, lid-closed mode armed, waiting for work"; 8: "Remi, lid-closed mode armed, waiting: battery low" (the refusal reason, from the hub); 9: "Remi, keeping this Mac awake with the lid closed, until 14:30"; 11: "Remi, lid-closed mode stopped after an error".
  Whether the status item exposes the label as its name and a value separately to VoiceOver, and how `MenuBarExtra` forwards `.accessibilityValue`, is UNVERIFIED (today's code sets only `.accessibilityLabel`, `MacNotificationCoordinator.swift:300`); check with VoiceOver on a real Mac.
- A tooltip with the same sentence plus the machine's name; a spoken announcement when level 2 starts or ends (an `NSAccessibility` announcement; UNVERIFIED) and the macOS notification of plan Q11.
- Reduce Motion: no animation is proposed in the bar. If an "armed" pulse is added later, read `accessibilityDisplayShouldReduceMotion` (or the SwiftUI environment value) and show a static mark instead.
- Increase Contrast: the template glyph follows the system; the 55 percent alpha of the hub-down "r" should become 100 percent when `accessibilityDisplayShouldIncreaseContrast` is true (the dash pattern carries the meaning), and nothing in the bar may depend on alpha alone.
- VISUAL-ACCEPTANCE.md names an iPhone simulator, Accessibility 5 text, Reduce Transparency and Reduce Motion for app screens. A menu bar item has no Dynamic Type and no translucency of its own (its popover does), so the acceptance for the icon must add its own evidence: a screenshot of the real bar per state in light and dark, at standard and at Increase Contrast, and a VoiceOver recording of the spoken value. This is a gap in the gate, to be written into the implementation spec.

## 7. The menu behind the icon

Style: keep `.window` (the cards need it; HIG exception noted in section 3). Layout, top to bottom, all existing rows kept:

1. Header: the existing needs-you header and count (`MacNeedsYouHeader`).
2. The question cards, or the existing empty state.
3. A "This Mac" section, new:
   - Hub row: state in words ("Running on 127.0.0.1:18765", "Not reachable", "Needs an update: remi 0.8.1, this app needs protocol 2") and the action: Start (when no hub, only if research A finds a sandbox-legal way), Open Machines (for approval states).
   - Keep-awake row: state in words and the numbers that matter ("Level 1: holding, 2 sessions working"; "Level 2: armed"; "Level 2: on, lid closed possible until 14:30, battery 62 percent"; "Level 2: waiting, battery 28 percent, needs 35"; "Level 2: stopped after an error, details") and the actions below.
4. Footer: Open Remi, Settings... (Command-comma), Quit.

Per state:

| State | Hub row | Keep-awake row | Actions shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Not reachable" (or "Not running", only if the hub answers refused versus timed out, UNVERIFIED) | hidden | Start hub (if allowed), Open Machines |
| 2 | hidden | hidden | the identity error and Try again (as today) |
| 3 | running | "Mac may sleep" (level 1 off or idle), or hidden | none |
| 4, 6 | running | "Level 1 holding" | none (nothing to change; level 1 is configuration) |
| 5 | running | as above | answer in place (as today) |
| 7 | running | "Level 2 armed" | Disarm |
| 8 | running | "Level 2 armed, waiting: <reason>" | Disarm |
| 9, 10 | running | "Level 2 on until 14:30" | Stop, Extend 1 hour |
| 11 | running | "Stopped after an error: <reason>" | Arm again, Details |
| 12 | running | "Level 2 disarmed" | Arm |

Arm, Disarm, Stop and Extend are requests to the hub, not local actions: the sandboxed app holds no privilege and cannot run `pmset` (`Remi.entitlements`), and they need the hub's authorization, which the plan puts behind `allow_phone = true` (L2-APP, plan.md line 131; ADR 0050 line 146).
When `allow_phone` is false the actions are not drawn (DESIGN.md principle 3, never show a control that cannot work) and the row says "Change on the Mac with remi keep-awake".
The Settings window (research of the other agents, #1805 part 2) owns the keys; the menu only shows state and the few actions above.
Two things in the menu depend on the other research agents: whether a Start hub action may exist (sandbox), and what the Settings window writes.

## 10. What could not be verified

- Nothing was seen on a real menu bar: no app launch, no simulator, no screenshot of the bar. Legibility is judged from `rsvg-convert` at 36 px.
- The status item's maximum width, two-line text support, `MenuBarExtra` label redraw and animation, and the wallpaper-tinted bar behavior (no Apple page states them).
- Whether `NSStatusBar.system.thickness` (22.0 in a command-line process here) is 22 or 24 on a notch Mac, against the HIG's "24 pt".
- The system color RGB values in B (recalled), the WCAG 3 to 1 non-text threshold (recalled), SF Pro's cap-height ratio (recalled), and the SF Symbols' meanings.
- Whether VoiceOver reads a status item's value separately from its label, and whether announcements work from a `MenuBarExtra`.
- Whether the hub's refused connect (not running) can be told from a timeout (unreachable) through `MachineConnectionStatus`; today one `disconnected` or `unavailable(reason:)` is all the app has.
- Behavior of the system when it hides a wide item on a crowded bar or a notch Mac.
- Level 2 states: the plan is not built (L2-POL, L2-CORE, L2-HUB, L2-APP are cards, `c7ab6f51`; the branch head has moved since, without changes to the states), so every level 2 label and refusal reason is the plan's, not shipped code.
